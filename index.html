Admission Logic & Queuing System Control

Select and compare the 3 dynamic patient admission options to govern resource allocation and triage queue behavior.

Active: Priority Wise
Option 1

Priority Wise

Dynamically ranks patients using a multi-factor score: Clinical Severity (L1-L5), Wait Time Escalation, and Specialist Doctor Matching.

Urgency Factor = (Severity × 0.5) + (Disease × 0.35) + (Age × 0.15)
  • Prevents mortality in high-acuity cases
  • Escalates waiting patients over time
  • Optimal for Emergency & ICU triage
Active Strategy
Option 2

First Come First Serve (FCFS)

Strict FIFO (First-In, First-Out) queuing system. Patients are admitted strictly in the order they arrive, regardless of medical severity or resource needs.

Formula: Wait Time (Descending)
  • Ensures maximum waiting fairness
  • Prevents low-severity starvation
  • May increase mortality for critical cases
Inactive
Option 3

Least Resources First (LRF)

Prioritizes patients who consume the lowest resource footprint (shortest treatment duration, fewest nurses, no OR or ICU bed requirement).

Formula: Min (Time + Nurses + OR/ICU Overhead)
  • Maximizes patient discharge throughput
  • Clears quick consultations rapidly
  • De-prioritizes high-resource ICU cases
Inactive

Triage Priority Scale & Dynamic Mortality Guide

Hospital Resource Allocation & Dynamic Mortality Engine Documentation

1. Dynamic Priority Points Calculation Engine

Every queued patient is dynamically evaluated on each simulation tick using a weighted score formula. Higher scores move patients to the front of the triage queue:

Urgency Factor = (Severity × 0.5) + (Disease × 0.35) + (Age × 0.15)
Positive Score Factors
  • Severity: contributes Severity × 0.5.
  • Disease: contributes Disease × 0.35; the condition severity is used when no separate disease score is supplied.
  • Age: contributes Age × 0.15. Higher urgency-factor values sort first under Priority Wise.
Negative Adjustments & Offsets
  • Staffing/resource events: affect admission capacity and treatment duration; they do not change the urgency formula.

2. Dynamic Mortality System Protocol

Patients held in triage past a 2-minute initial waiting grace period accumulate dynamic mortality risk in 10-minute intervals. Mortality risk is calculated separately from the urgency factor and is driven by triage severity and waiting time:

Level 5 (Critical / Resus)
25% Base
+5% every 10 min (after 2m)
Level 4 (Severe / Life-Th)
15% Base
+5% every 10 min (after 2m)
Level 3 (High / Emergent)
10% Base
+5% every 10 min (after 2m)

* Note: Non-critical patients (L1 & L2) experience baseline zero or minimal mortality risk (+1% every 10 min after 2m), maintaining primary focus on high-acuity resuscitation.

3. Staffing, Peak Hours & Resource Events

Normal Staffing Allocation
  • L1: 1 nurse, no doctor.
  • L2–L3: 1 specialist doctor (or available normal/cross-department doctor where allowed) + 1 nurse.
  • L4–L5: 1 specialist doctor + 2 nurses.
  • Default nurse pool: 15; the Base Configuration field can increase or decrease it.
Staff Shortage
  • Reduces each doctor and nurse pool's effective capacity by 20%.
  • L1 remains nurse-led.
  • L2–L3 can receive assistance from another department's available doctor.
  • L4–L5 still require the appropriate specialist.
Resource Failure
  • ICU beds and ORs become unavailable.
  • Surgery/treatment duration increases by 30%.
  • Doctor pools are not reduced by Resource Failure.
Peak Hour System
  • Runs automatically every 3 simulation hours.
  • Each peak period lasts 15 simulation minutes, not real-world minutes.
  • During peak, random walking-customer arrivals increase from every 2–4 simulation minutes to every 1–2 simulation minutes.
  • The current state is shown beside the simulation clock and can be switched OFF with the Peak Hours button.

3. Hospital Efficiency Index Calculation

The overall Hospital Efficiency Percentage represents operational throughput, survival rates, and triage promptness:

Hospital Efficiency = Math.max(0, 100% - (Mortality Rate × 2.5) - (Avg Queue Wait Mins × 0.8))
